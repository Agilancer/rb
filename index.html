REALISTIC BEATUP

Pick something from the bottom menu, then click in the world to drop it. Keep clicking to drop more!

Left mouse: use the selected tool (Grab is the default — drag and fling anything). Right mouse: use the power you picked last (starts as Punch). Middle mouse / arrow keys: move camera. Wheel: zoom.

Space pause   G slow-mo   H heal everyone   Shift+R reset room   Z undo   X delete under mouse   F freeze under mouse   Q/E spin what you hold   F1 this help

Tools on the left: weld things together, tie ropes, pin, freeze, grow, shrink, clone, set on fire, zap, shoot and more. Machines switch on and off with the Switch tool.
