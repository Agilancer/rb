REALISTIC BEATUP

The panel on the left holds everything. ITEMS has three tabs: TOOL (hands, blocks, toys, weapons, machines), POWER (powers, elements, hazards) and ENTITY (characters). WORLD has maps, gravity and disasters. OPTIONS has gore, brawl, save and the 3D mode.

Pick a thing, then click in the room to drop it. Click a character to see their body status, heart monitor, blood, pain and temperature at the top right.

Left mouse: use the selected tool (Grab by default: drag and fling). Right mouse: use the power you picked last. Middle mouse / arrow keys: move camera. Wheel: zoom.

Space pause   G slow-mo   H heal everyone   R reset room   Z undo   X delete under mouse   F freeze under mouse   Q/E spin what you hold   F1 help

Scissors cut limbs at any joint (drag to slice) and cut ropes. Rope ties to limbs and necks; pull hard enough and they tear. Hard hits bruise and break bones, sharp things cut, explosions dismember.
