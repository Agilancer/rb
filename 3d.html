REALISTIC BEATUP 3D

You are Martlebrough. The town is quiet, the hills are huge, and there are cannons. Fix all of that.

WASD / left stickMove. Shift / RT sprints and barges people over.
Mouse / right stickLook. The camera swings in behind you when you run. Wheel zooms.
Space / AJump; tap again in the air to flip. Hold for higher jumps.
Left click / XCombo: jab, hook, launcher. Hits lock onto whoever is in front of you.
Right click / E / RBShoulder charge: flattens people, fells trees, wrecks cars.
C / BRoll (you can't be run over mid-roll). Press it just before you land to roll out of any fall.
SwordsWalk over a glowing sword to pick it up: slashes take limbs off. T drops it.
In a cannonSteer with WASD, Space bails out, click to dive-bomb.
F / YTongue-grab a person or thing; click to throw it.
Q / LB   B / LTGrenade  /  jetpack
X G R MRagdoll   slow-mo   reset   big map

Walk into a cannon to get fired across the map: Bellmourn Village, Splinter Hamlet, Yeet Summit and Boulder Farm each have one to fire you home. Red barrels explode, cars don't brake, the boulders on the ledge above the farm roll, and the graveharts in the meadows really don't like being punched.
