GOATSVILLE

A destruction sandbox. Everything breaks.

You are the golden goat. The town has towers up to 12 storeys, schools, a bank, a police station, a grocery store, houses and three parks full of physics toys. Every wall, floor and car can be blown apart. Monsters roam: kobolds, goblins, zombies, giants, a werewolf and rival goats. Villagers mostly just want to live.

On foot · WASD run (camera-relative) · Shift sprint · Space jump, again to front-flip · hold B jetpack · Wheel camera distance

Goat stuff · E headbutt (sprint into it for a charge) · hold C lick and drag anything, let go to throw it · R go limp (ragdoll), again to get up · V sword swing or kick

Guns · hold RMB to aim over the shoulder · LMB fire what's in your hands · pistols, rifles, shotguns, snipers, grenades, C4 and one nuke are lying around town

Cars · F jack the nearest car (the driver gets thrown out) or get out · W/S gas and reverse · A/D steer · Space handbrake drift · Shift boost · LMB fire mounts. Bail out at speed for a tumble.

Heat · hurting locals, jacking cars and fighting cops raises your 🚨 level. Cops on foot come first, then cruisers. Get out of sight and they lose you.

Tab gear screen: a weapon in each hand. Weapon pickups glow gold all over town; walk over one with full hands to swap. · G slow-mo · H help
